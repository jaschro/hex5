How to play

  • Players take turns placing one stone on any empty cell. White goes first.
  • Win by getting five or more in a row along any of the three hex directions.
  • Capture by flanking exactly two enemy stones in a line: ● ○ ○ ●. The two are removed.
  • Placing a stone into a flanked position is safe; captures only happen on the capturer's move.
  • You also win by capturing five pairs.
